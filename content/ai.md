## Principles

1. **Ask the tool the question you would ask a consultant, then check the citation.** Synthesis tools are good at assembling; they are not a substitute for reading the source.
2. **Never enter patient identifiers** into a tool that is not covered by your institution's agreements.
3. **Treat a confident answer with no citation as a hypothesis**, not a finding.
4. **Use AI to widen the differential, not to close it.** Its best use in reasoning is as a prompt for what you have not yet considered.
