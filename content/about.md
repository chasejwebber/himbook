**HIMbook** (Hospital Internal Medicine book) is a curated, public resource for learners and teachers of inpatient internal medicine.

## Editor

[Chase J. Webber, DO, FACP](https://chasejwebber.com) — Associate Professor of Clinical Medicine and Biomedical Informatics, Vanderbilt University Medical Center; academic hospitalist; director of the "How Doctors Think" Integrated Science Course.

## How resources are chosen

Everything here is curated, not comprehensive. A resource earns a spot by being the thing a resident should open *first* for that question. Open resources are listed ahead of licensed ones so the page is useful to readers outside VUMC.

**Legend:** 🔓 open to anyone · 🔒 VUMC login (Eskind/CKM licensed) · 🏥 VUMC internal network only

To suggest an addition or report a broken link, [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/chasejwebber/himbook/issues). Contributions from residents and faculty are welcome; see the repository's `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
