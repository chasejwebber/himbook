> **HIMbook** = *Hospital Internal Medicine* book. For teachers and learners of inpatient clinical reasoning. The HIMbook front page brings together the educational and decision-support resources that hospitalists and teaching teams actually need, all in one place.

**Legend:** 🔓 open to anyone · 🔒 VUMC login (Eskind/CKM licensed) · 🏥 VUMC internal network only

*From the Editor: everything here is curated, but does not claim to be comprehensive. A resource earns a spot by being the thing a resident should open first for that question. Suggest an addition → [GitHub issue](https://github.com/chasejwebber/himbook/issues).*

---

## 1. What's your question? - Point of care

The first-click references for a clinical question on the wards.

- 🔓 [VLRChat](https://vlr.chat) — "What does the Handbook say?" An AI educational decision support system by and for Vanderbilt residents. (Based on the 7th Edition IM Housestaff Handbook)
- 🔓 [OpenEvidence](https://openevidence.com) — GenAI Medical Knowledge Platform 
- 🔒 [UpToDate](https://researchguides.library.vanderbilt.edu/uptodate) — includes UpToDate Expert AI; use the *Hospital Medicine* table of contents
- 🔒 [ClinicalKey](https://myknowledgehub.vumc.org/ckmres?rid=35851) — Elsevier texts, Clinical Overviews, Procedures Consult
- 🔒 [BMJ Best Practice](https://myknowledgehub.vumc.org/ckmres?rid=5913) — good second opinion when UpToDate is equivocal
- 🔒 AccessMedicine — Harrison's, CMDT, Diagnosaurus 
- 🔒 VisualDx 
- 🔓 [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/) — plain-language explanations to use with patients
- 🔓 [DermNet](https://dermnetnz.org/) — rash lookup
- 🔓 [Life in the Fast Lane ECG Library](https://litfl.com/ecg-library/)
- 🔓 [Radiopaedia](https://radiopaedia.org/) — imaging findings and cases
