The Four-Square Prioritized Differential (FSPD) sorts a differential along two axes — **likelihood** and **consequence of missing it** — so that "must not miss" diagnoses are handled explicitly rather than buried in a long list.

|                       | Likely                       | Unlikely                        |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Dangerous**         | Work up now                  | Rule out with a targeted test   |
| **Not dangerous**     | Treat empirically / observe  | Defer                           |
